Contribute a route

Help thousands of commuters get where they're going. A route is one origin-to-destination journey, broken into ordered steps — each with a transport mode (taxi, keke, bike, walk), a landmark, and a short instruction.

1. Sign in

Verify your WhatsApp number so we can credit you when your route is approved.

2. Map it out

List the bus stops, junctions, and landmarks along the way. Add a step for each leg.

3. Submit for review

Our reviewers verify the route. Once approved, your account is credited.
